Privacy Policy – YT Sort

This Privacy Policy explains how YT Sort uses YouTube API Services and how data from your YouTube account is accessed, used, and retained.

Use of YouTube API Services

YT Sort uses the YouTube Data API to read information about your playlists and videos in order to display and sort them according to your chosen options.

YT Sort does not upload content, post comments, or otherwise modify your YouTube channel. Access is limited to the read-only scopes you approve on the Google consent screen.

Data obtained from the YouTube API Services is used only to provide the functionality of the YT Sort tool and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Applicability of Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms

YT Sort uses YouTube API Services. By using this tool, you agree that your use of YouTube is also governed by:

  • The Google Privacy Policy .
  • The YouTube Terms of Service .

Data collected and processed

When you authorise access via Google OAuth, YT Sort may access the following types of data from your YouTube account, depending on the scopes you grant:

  • Playlist metadata (for example: titles, IDs, positions, item counts).
  • Video metadata needed for sorting (for example: titles, IDs, durations, statistics).
